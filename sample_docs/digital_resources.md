# Digital Resources and Services

## E-Books and Audiobooks

Access thousands of e-books and audiobooks through our digital lending platforms:

### OverDrive/Libby
- Over 15,000 titles available
- Compatible with most e-readers, tablets, and smartphones
- Automatic return - no late fees
- Download the Libby app or visit riverside.overdrive.com

### Hoopla
- Instant borrowing - no waiting lists
- Includes e-books, audiobooks, music, movies, and TV shows
- Limit: 8 items per month per cardholder
- Access at hoopladigital.com

## Digital Magazines

Access over 100 popular magazines through RBdigital. Read current and back issues on your device. No checkout limits.

## Online Databases

Library cardholders have free access to:

### Research Databases
- **Academic Search Complete**: Scholarly articles across all disciplines
- **JSTOR**: Academic journals and primary sources
- **Gale PowerSearch**: News, magazines, and reference content

### Learning Resources
- **Rosetta Stone**: Learn languages online (limit: 2 hours per day)
- **Lynda.com/LinkedIn Learning**: Professional development courses
- **Tutor.com**: Live homework help (available 2 PM - 11 PM daily)

### Local History
- **Riverside Historical Archives**: Digitized newspapers, photos, and documents from 1880-present

## Tech Lending

Library cardholders can check out technology for home use:

- **Laptops**: 1-week loan period
- **Tablets**: 1-week loan period
- **Mobile Hotspots**: 2-week loan period
- **E-readers**: 3-week loan period

Borrowers must be 18+ and have a library card in good standing for at least 6 months. A signed technology lending agreement is required.

## Digital Library Card

Get instant access to digital resources with our Digital Library Card option. Apply online and receive temporary access within 24 hours. Visit the library within 30 days to convert to a full physical card.
