# Acme Widgets Employee Handbook

## Company Overview

Acme Widgets is a fictional manufacturing company specializing in high-quality widgets for various applications. Founded in 1995, we pride ourselves on innovation and employee satisfaction.

## Working Hours

Standard working hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Flexible working arrangements are available upon approval from your manager. Remote work is permitted up to two days per week for eligible positions.

## Leave Policy

Employees accrue vacation leave at a rate of 15 days per year. Sick leave is provided at 10 days annually. All leave requests must be submitted at least two weeks in advance through the HR portal, except for emergency sick leave.

## Benefits

Full-time employees are eligible for comprehensive health insurance coverage starting on their first day of employment. Dental and vision coverage is available after a 90-day probationary period. The company matches 401(k) contributions up to 5% of salary.

## Professional Development

Acme Widgets supports continuous learning. Employees can request up to $1,500 annually for professional development courses, conferences, or certifications related to their role. All requests must be approved by the department manager.

## Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to treat colleagues with respect and professionalism. Discrimination, harassment, or bullying of any kind will not be tolerated. Please report any concerns to the HR department immediately.

## Performance Reviews

Performance reviews are conducted annually in December. Employees and managers collaborate to set goals for the upcoming year. Merit-based salary increases are determined following these reviews.
