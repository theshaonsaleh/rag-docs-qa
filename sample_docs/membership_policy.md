# Library Membership Policy

## Who Can Join

Any resident of Riverside County can obtain a free library card. Non-residents may purchase an annual membership for $50.

## How to Register

To register for a library card, you must:
1. Complete a registration form (available online or at the library)
2. Provide proof of current address (utility bill, lease agreement, or driver's license)
3. Present a valid photo ID

Children under 13 must have a parent or guardian sign their registration form.

## Card Privileges

Library card holders can:
- Borrow up to 25 items at a time
- Reserve items online
- Access digital resources and databases from home
- Use computers and WiFi in the library
- Attend all library programs and events

## Loan Periods

- **Books**: 3 weeks (renewable twice if no holds)
- **DVDs and audiobooks**: 1 week (renewable once)
- **New releases**: 1 week (no renewals)
- **Magazines**: 1 week (no renewals)

## Fines and Fees

The library operates on a fine-free system for most materials. However, replacement fees apply for lost or damaged items. The cost is the current retail price of the item plus a $5 processing fee.
