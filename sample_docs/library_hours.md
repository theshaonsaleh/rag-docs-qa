# Riverside Public Library - Hours of Operation

## Regular Hours

The Riverside Public Library is open to serve the community:

- **Monday - Thursday**: 9:00 AM - 8:00 PM
- **Friday - Saturday**: 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM
- **Sunday**: 1:00 PM - 5:00 PM

## Holiday Closures

The library will be closed on the following holidays:
- New Year's Day
- Independence Day
- Thanksgiving Day
- Christmas Day

## Summer Hours

During the summer months (June 1 - August 31), the library extends its weekend hours:
- **Saturday**: 9:00 AM - 7:00 PM
- **Sunday**: 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM

## Special Services Hours

### Children's Story Time
Tuesdays and Thursdays at 10:30 AM (during the school year)

### Computer Lab
Available during all regular library hours; reservations recommended for sessions longer than 1 hour.
