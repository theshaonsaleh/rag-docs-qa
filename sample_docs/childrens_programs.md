# Children's Programs and Services

## Story Time Programs

### Baby & Toddler Time (Ages 0-2)
Wednesdays at 10:00 AM
A lap-sit program featuring songs, rhymes, and board books. Designed to introduce the youngest library users to the joy of stories and books.

### Preschool Story Time (Ages 3-5)
Tuesdays and Thursdays at 10:30 AM
Stories, songs, and crafts to develop early literacy skills and prepare children for school.

### Family Story Time (All Ages)
Saturdays at 11:00 AM
A fun story time for the whole family! Stories, music, and movement activities.

## Summer Reading Program

Join our annual Summer Reading Challenge (June-August)!
- Track your reading online or with a paper log
- Earn prizes for reading milestones
- Attend special weekly programs and events
- Grand prize drawing at the end of summer

Open to children ages 0-12. Registration begins May 1st.

## STEAM Programs

### Saturday Science (Ages 7-12)
First Saturday of each month, 2:00 PM
Hands-on science experiments and activities exploring physics, chemistry, biology, and engineering.

### Coding Club (Ages 8-14)
Second and fourth Thursdays, 4:00 PM
Learn to code with Scratch, Python, and robotics kits.

## Homework Help

### After-School Zone (K-8th grade)
Monday-Thursday, 3:30-5:30 PM
Drop in for a quiet place to study with homework help available from volunteer tutors and library staff.

## Special Events

- Monthly author visits and book signings
- Holiday craft programs
- Read to a Dog program (second Sunday, 2-3 PM)
- Teen Advisory Board (ages 13-18, meets monthly)

## Children's Room

The Children's Room features:
- Picture books and early readers organized by theme
- Separate young adult section for teens
- Comfortable reading nooks and activity spaces
- Educational toys and puzzles
- Four child-friendly computer stations

Parents and caregivers are responsible for supervising children in the library at all times.
