# Meeting Room Reservation Policy

## Available Rooms

The Riverside Public Library offers three meeting spaces for community use:

### Community Room A
- Capacity: 50 people
- Features: Projector, screen, whiteboard, tables and chairs
- Ideal for: Workshops, presentations, larger meetings

### Community Room B
- Capacity: 20 people
- Features: Flat-screen TV with HDMI connection, conference table
- Ideal for: Small group meetings, study groups

### Quiet Study Room
- Capacity: 8 people
- Features: Round table, whiteboard, quiet space
- Ideal for: Tutoring, small study groups, interviews

## Reservation Requirements

- Library card in good standing required
- Rooms must be reserved at least 48 hours in advance
- Maximum reservation: 3 hours per day
- Reservations can be made up to 30 days in advance

## Room Use Guidelines

**Permitted Uses:**
- Educational programs and classes
- Community organization meetings
- Book clubs and discussion groups
- Study groups
- Non-profit activities

**Not Permitted:**
- Commercial activities or sales
- Fundraising events (except for library-sponsored programs)
- Religious services
- Political campaign events
- Private social events (birthdays, weddings, etc.)

## Fees

Meeting rooms are FREE for non-profit, educational, and community group use. However, a refundable deposit of $50 is required at the time of reservation and will be returned if the room is left clean and undamaged.

## Cancellation Policy

Please cancel reservations at least 24 hours in advance if you cannot attend. Groups that fail to show up for reservations twice within a 6-month period may lose meeting room privileges.
