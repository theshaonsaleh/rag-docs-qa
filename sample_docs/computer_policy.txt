RIVERSIDE PUBLIC LIBRARY - COMPUTER AND INTERNET USE POLICY

COMPUTER ACCESS
All library cardholders may use library computers for free. Guest passes are available for visitors (up to 3 per month). Computer sessions are limited to 2 hours per day, but may be extended if no one is waiting.

PRINTING AND COPYING
Black and white printing: $0.10 per page
Color printing: $0.50 per page
Scanning: Free (save to USB drive or email)

ACCEPTABLE USE
The library's computers and internet access are provided for educational, informational, and recreational purposes. Users must comply with all applicable laws and library policies.

PROHIBITED ACTIVITIES:
- Accessing illegal content
- Harassment or threatening behavior
- Attempting to damage or modify library systems
- Installing unauthorized software
- Excessive streaming during peak hours (please be considerate of other users)

WIFI ACCESS
Free WiFi is available throughout the library. No library card required. The network name is "Riverside_Library_WiFi" and no password is needed.

PERSONAL DEVICES
Users are welcome to use personal laptops and devices in the library. Power outlets are available at most tables. Please keep sound at a considerate level and use headphones for audio/video content.

TECHNICAL SUPPORT
Library staff can provide basic assistance with:
- Logging in and basic computer use
- Accessing library resources
- Basic Microsoft Office questions
- Printing and scanning

For advanced technical issues, we recommend scheduling an appointment with our Technology Help Desk (available Tuesday and Thursday afternoons, 2-4 PM).
