# Acme Widgets Product Catalog 2026

## Widget Series A

The Series A widget is our entry-level product, perfect for small-scale applications. Available in three sizes: compact (5cm), standard (10cm), and large (15cm). Each widget is crafted from durable polymer material and rated for up to 10,000 cycles.

**Pricing:**
- Compact: $25 per unit
- Standard: $40 per unit  
- Large: $55 per unit

**Colors:** Red, blue, green, yellow, and custom colors available for bulk orders (minimum 100 units).

## Widget Series B

The Series B widget represents our premium offering, designed for industrial applications requiring high durability. Manufactured from aerospace-grade aluminum alloy, these widgets are rated for 50,000 cycles and come with a 5-year warranty.

**Specifications:**
- Weight: 200g (standard model)
- Dimensions: 12cm x 8cm x 4cm
- Operating temperature range: -40°C to 150°C
- Price: $180 per unit

## Widget Series C

Our newest innovation, the Series C widget, incorporates smart technology. Each unit features embedded sensors that monitor performance metrics in real-time. Data can be accessed via our mobile app or web dashboard.

**Features:**
- Bluetooth 5.0 connectivity
- Battery life: 2 years (replaceable CR2032)
- Compatible with iOS and Android devices
- Cloud data storage included for first year
- Price: $250 per unit

All products include a standard 1-year warranty covering manufacturing defects. Extended warranties are available for purchase at checkout. Series B products include an extended 5-year warranty at no additional cost.
